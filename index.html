FAQ

Got Questions?

Can I choose who I get matched with?
You can specify preferences like preferred gender, age range, and shared interests in your profile. We use these to curate the best possible match — but the final pairing is our team's call, keeping serendipity alive.
Why does each café have a different QR code?
The QR code ties your profile to a specific location and time slot. This ensures you're only matched with people who'll actually be at the same café — no confused meetups.
What if I don't click with the person?
That's okay! You're not obligated to stay longer than your coffee. Most people find the shared situation makes conversation easy, even with very different personalities.
Is this a dating service?
No — it's a social connection experience. Romantic connections may happen naturally, but the primary goal is meaningful conversation and expanding your social circle.
When will more cities be available?
Hyderabad and Chennai are coming in Q3 2026. Mumbai and Pune by end of year. Sign up for updates and you'll be the first to know when your city goes live.
