Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. For the first 7 days there is no registration fee. You only pay for what you order at the café.

Can I choose who I meet?

Yes. You choose a gender preference and any number of groups, such as IT, doctors, police, lawyers, business owners, startup people, real estate, film industry (short film artists, directors, models), construction, students or others.

How will I know someone matches?

You see an alert on the 🔔 My matches button. Open it to view their profile and tap OK if you'd like to meet.

What does the café QR do?

It links you to that café. Scan it to register at that venue, and scan it again when you arrive to check in for your confirmed meetup.

Is this a dating service?

No. Coffee With Stranger is for conversations, friendships and networking in public cafés.
